Likely panel questions — both of you should answer all of them

  • Why drop Ellie's text but keep her timestamps? (Label leakage vs. response latency.)
  • What is PHQ-8, where does the cut-off of 10 come from, and what are its limits as a label?
  • Why split by subject and not by segment? What would go wrong otherwise?
  • Why UAR / F1 rather than accuracy? (≈ 30 % depressed class.)
  • Why would a graph help over mean-pooled features or a BiLSTM? Which edge types and why?
  • Which of your features differed significantly after multiple-comparison correction, and does the direction match the literature?
  • What is COVAREP, and why do we use voiced frames only for pitch statistics?
  • Why does the dataset's small size make your numbers unstable, and how do you plan to quantify that? (Repeated CV, bootstrap CIs.)
